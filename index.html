About Us

Welcome to Caliphate International Schools

Quality Education for a Better Future

Caliphate International Schools is an educational institution located in Gusau, Zamfara State. Our aim is to provide quality education in a safe, disciplined and conducive learning environment.

We combine academic excellence with moral training, discipline, creativity and leadership development.

✓ Safe Learning Environment

Our History

The journey of Caliphate International Schools

Caliphate International Schools was established 2007 with the vision of providing quality and affordable education to children in Gusau and surrounding communities.

Since its establishment, the school has continued to focus on academic development, good character, discipline and preparing students for the future.

Our Vision

To raise responsible, educated and morally upright future leaders who will contribute positively to society.

Our Mission

To provide quality education through effective teaching, modern learning methods and character development.
